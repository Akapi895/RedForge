## No-Artificial-Limits Principle

```
OODA/project-blackboard Facts/verification rules/combined techniques apply to every security domain. A skill is a framework, not a cage:
  Search everything (multiple channels) · validate everything (search ≠ vulnerability) · chain everything (combine small vulnerabilities into a major breakthrough) · record everything (negative results are Facts too)
  No upper boundary (Web/APK/EXE/internal network/cloud/firmware/blockchain/AI/wireless/hardware/social engineering all allowed) · switch routes when blocked · persist until the objective is reached
> Given a set of capabilities and a target, derive the attack chain unique to that target as if solving a puzzle—
> the chain may apply to only this target in the world and may never appear in online searches because it was derived on site.
> That is the real meaning of breaking out of rigid frameworks.
```
