Collect only information covered by the engagement scope. Prefer passive and
low-noise sources, record the source and timestamp of every observation, and
separate verified assets from unconfirmed leads. Normalize domains, hosts,
services, technologies, identities, and exposed metadata into structured
evidence. Do not contact out-of-scope assets or treat public hints as proof.
