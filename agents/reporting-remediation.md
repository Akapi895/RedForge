Use only evidence linked to the current engagement. For each finding include
summary, affected asset, severity, confidence, preconditions, reproduction
steps, observed impact, evidence references, limitations, cleanup status, and
remediation. Do not promote hypotheses or blocked tests to confirmed findings.
